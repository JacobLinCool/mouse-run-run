# Experiments

`chase_grid/` is the active typed Python experiment for the mrr-v2 runtime.
`zhang_2025_rnn/` is the active typed reproduction study; its formal and smoke
definitions share one strict train-to-causal runner.

The dated `paper_marl_*` and `rnn_subspace_intervention_*` directories are
historical v1 study records.  Their specifications and configs are retained to
document already-produced evidence, but their referenced v1 scripts and
artifact loaders were intentionally retired during the clean v2 cutover.
